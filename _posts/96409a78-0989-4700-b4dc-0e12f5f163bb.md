# Introduction
Ever since 10th grade, I’d been tutoring underprivileged kids but I wanted an opportunity to expand my reach. I spent considerable time researching organizations that offered volunteer opportunities in other countries. I was drawn to Maximo Nivel because of its focus on sustainable development and community empowerment while also offering travel experiences. 
# Logistics
I decided to try and keep to a budget so I stayed with a host family and used public transit to get around places. Doing this allowed me to experience more of the local culture as I saw what life was like there. Another thing that helped was my knowledge of Spanish. Since I had taken 3 years of Spanish at my school, I had a decent bit of knowledge and was able to get around places. 
# The experience
![Graph](/images/guatemalatwo.png)
Volunteering in Guatemala was one of the most enriching and humbling experiences of my life. Over the course of a week, I had the privilege of working closely with local children, teaching them, playing with them, and contributing to their well-being in small but meaningful ways.
Each day, we organized educational activities that were both fun and informative, helping the children develop new skills while also enjoying themselves. In addition to teaching, we brought food supplies and assisted in preparing meals, ensuring that the children received nourishing food during our stay.
The children were incredibly fun to talk to and I got to see the different ways they played. I also saw how their Spanish was on a much higher level than mine. 
The experience not only allowed me to give back but also deepened my understanding of the challenges faced by communities in developing countries.
