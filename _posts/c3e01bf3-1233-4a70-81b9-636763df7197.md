# Introduction
Being selected for this program was a result of my performance in the summer research program, where I first demonstrated my passion for problem-solving and my ability to tackle challenging concepts. The knowledge and experience I gained during the Wolfram Emerging Leaders Program have been invaluable in shaping my academic and professional aspirations.
Participating in the Wolfram Emerging Leaders Program was an incredible opportunity that allowed me to dive deep into research and collaborate with like-minded peers. This semester-long experience challenged me to think critically and work creatively with my team on a project that blended our diverse interests and skills.

# The Project
Our project began with extensive research on video game box art, which laid the foundation for a detailed paper we co-authored. The process of gathering and synthesizing information, debating ideas, and refining our arguments was both rigorous and rewarding. One of the highlights of the program was generating visualizations using Wolfram tools, which brought our data to life and allowed us to present complex concepts in an engaging way.
We were able to map various image analysis functions onto these images to obtain various pieces of data such as the foreground vs background split and the color distribution.

Using the data we got from analyzing the color distribution, we were able to make a circle packing function to create the visualization below of the different colors in the box art. 
![Graph](/images/circlepacking.png)
Additionally, using a variational auto encoder, we trained a model to recreate video game box art. The result below was generated after training on older box art but it still bears some resemblance to classic game box art. 
![Graph](/images/VAE.png)
Overall, this was a great and productive experience.
